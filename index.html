Every decision is a trade-off. See the forces before you choose.

A visual collection of the trade-offs that keep coming up in projects, software, data, data science and communication. Each one comes with an animation that shows how the factors push against each other, and a short guide to using it in your next decision.

How to use these models

Name the factors. Most arguments about plans are really arguments about which factor gets to move. Saying "we're holding the deadline fixed" makes the conversation honest.

Decide what's locked. Each animation lets you hold one factor constant and watch the others react. In a real decision, agree on what's fixed before debating the rest.

Look for the curve. Many trade-offs aren't linear. Waiting time, coordination cost and precision all have a knee where a little more of one thing costs a lot more of another.

These are simplified models for framing a decision, not precise forecasts. Use them to ask better questions.
